## High-resolution generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: HIGH RESOLUTION production first-person game horizon texture. Native wide 3:1 panoramic canvas, 3072x1024 or larger, at least 2048 pixels wide. New authored high resolution artwork, not upscaling.
Primary request: a believable distant industrial city skyline for classic Doom/Duke inspired outdoor courtyards, usable also in restrained sci-fi and clean cartoon settings. Continuous unwrapped 360-degree panorama.
Composition: the horizon is precisely the horizontal middle. An irregular long skyline made of many modest industrial buildings: low warehouse roofs, stepped factory blocks, small pitched sheds, varied chimneys, a few utility frames, layered distant rooftops. No huge skyscrapers. Rooftops mostly 5–12 degrees above the horizon; only a few slender chimneys reach about 15 degrees. On a 1024px-high canvas, most rooflines lie between y=445 and y=510 with rare small thin structures up to y=420.
Atmospheric depth: softly desaturated and slightly lighter far buildings behind darker mid-distance structures; subdued naturally shaded masonry, weathered metal roofing and small restrained structural details. Semi-realistic matte painted game environment background, controlled contrast, neutral cool gray/slate palette, quiet diffuse daylight. Clean credible silhouettes, good variation in roof shapes, no repetitive identical block icons.
Below the horizon: continuous fully opaque dark gray base down to the bottom, intentionally simple and unobtrusive. Do not depict a giant near-camera wall or vast close facade. Front buildings remain small and distant. Add only six to ten tiny subtle warm amber window/light dots among the mid-distance roofline across the whole panorama, each at most a few pixels, never huge glowing windows or neon.
Horizontal repeat: outermost 8 percent on both ends are quiet matching low roof/ground bands, identical flat silhouette height at the horizon and matching dark base gray. No chimneys, unique towers or bright window dots near the seam. Left and right columns should match exactly, transparent above the same roofline and opaque below.
Transparency: genuine alpha-zero empty sky above all skyline silhouettes; antialiased architectural edges; alpha=255 solid buildings and base. No sky fill, stars, clouds, sun, moon or fake checkerboard.
Avoid: text, watermark, brand signs, recognizable landmarks, cars, people, trees, smoke, cartoon blobs, black outlines, wide-angle fisheye, foreground perspective, huge front facades, dazzling night-city brightness or a vertical seam stripe.

Exact generation prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original RGBA grass ground-cover patch albedo for two crossed cards in a 3D tropical game.
Primary request: create a small airy but richly overlapping mound of meadow grass, not a rectangle and not an isolated single-root ornamental tuft. About 80–110 slender curved green blades emerging from FIVE loose overlapping root groups distributed across the central 65–70% of the width. Every root group is individually visible through natural open spaces between blades. Wispy blades fan gently outward from those multiple bases to occupy almost the full width around one-third height, while the outer sides are strongly feathered and sparse all the way down.
Composition: frontal orthographic view at grass level. Compact rounded irregular hill-shaped silhouette with tallest blades just off center, shorter root groups at both outer sides. Left side should be especially irregular and low, right side slightly taller but soft. No upright side boundary anywhere, no squared corners. Roots distributed horizontally, never converging into one tiny root point. Main five root groups meet a gently irregular baseline with several small notches between them. Rounded side contour tapers inward and becomes TRANSPARENT at the lower left and lower right corners.
Coverage: visible genuine transparent windows THROUGH the entire grass patch, including middle and lower half, approximately as much open air as green leaves. Many clear irregular holes between overlapping blades. A little layering is good, but no opaque mass. Uppermost quarter has only a handful of fine individual waving tips. Middle has a lacy roughly half-filled arrangement of overlapping blades. Lower half has slightly fuller green root groups interrupted by transparent vertical slits and uneven openings. No solid leaf body, no rectangular tuft mass, no near-solid hedge.
Style: realistically painted diffuse game texture, natural slightly muted medium olive-green with fresh sage-green blades and subtle yellow-green tips. Fine tapered blades with softly visible ribs, slight color variation, broadest individual blades about 1/80 image width. No excessive dark bases, no black shadows. Mostly living grass, a few pale curved blades, no dense brown thatch. Neutral even lighting with no directional highlights, no cast shadows, no painted ground.
Background: ACTUAL transparent alpha, no background color, checkerboard, floor or soil.
Constraints: every blade inside frame with a small transparent margin; only grass blades, no seedheads, large leaves, flowers, berries, ferns, rocks, text, watermark or border. Avoid flat-top hedges, rectangular grass cross-sections, lawn squares and any straight vertical side edge. This texture becomes crossed vertical cards, so the boundary must dissolve into disconnected blades on ALL sides.
```

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable ocean albedo texture for a Far Cry 1 inspired tropical coastal game. Primary request: one square flat orthographic top-down bitmap of calm blue-green tropical seawater, with very subtle low-contrast irregular broad ripple shading. Muted deep turquoise teal base; avoid fluorescent cyan. Naturally uneven, overlapping ripples at varied angles and sizes, with no regular grid, no diamond/checker weave, no repeated straight parallel stripes. Quiet low contrast diffuse color only: animated normals, reflection and shoreline foam are added by the engine. Truly seamless repeating edges horizontally and vertically, balanced color throughout with no large centered feature, no directional illumination gradient. No shore, sand, coral, seabed, rocks, objects, sky, horizon, sun glitter, white highlights, foam or breaking waves. No text or borders. Original game texture, not extracted from any game.

Runtime uses a 12 m world-anchored tile rotated 17 degrees, gentle surface
displacement, flowing UVs and terrain-mapped shoreline foam. The original
`ocean.png` remains available; no original game artwork is redistributed.
